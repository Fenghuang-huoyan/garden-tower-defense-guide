## Sort order: verified max DPS only, nothing else

This page ranks units strictly by their stated max DPS number from the wiki — no cost, no
obtainability weighting, just raw output. For a ranking that balances DPS against what it costs
to field, see [Best Units](/best-units/garden-tower-defense-best-units) instead; that page uses
a different sort and will show a different order.

Garden Tower Defense has 353 named units. 23 have been individually verified against the wiki
so far (see the shared data pool this site maintains internally) — this page ranks the
currently-obtainable ones among them and will grow as more get checked.

## Ranked by verified max DPS (currently obtainable units only)

| Rank | Unit | Max DPS | How to get it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Venus Flytrap | 3750 | Enchanted Summon crate, 0.01% chance |
| 2 | Bouncing Shroom | 3200 (AoE/splash) | Greenhouse Summon, 0.005% chance |
| 3 | Rafflesia | 1500 (trap-type, attaches to mobs) | Mystery Trader shop, 20% stock chance |
| 4 | Beehive | ~900 (computed: 2250 damage ÷ 2.5s interval; wiki doesn't state DPS directly) | Bee Summon, 0.01% chance |
| 5 | Confusion Plant | 734 (turns enemies back around the track) | Tropical Summon, 0.01% chance |
| 6 | Laser Plant | 667 (burst multiplier attack) | Enchanted Summon, 0.99% chance |
| 6 | Broccoli | 667 | Classic Summon, 1% chance |
| 8 | Palm Tree | 454 | Classic Summon, 4–5% chance |

**Venus Flytrap has the highest verified DPS among units you can actually still get (3750).**
No longer-obtainable unit is included in this ranking — see below for why that matters.

## Units checked but excluded from this ranking

Money Tree, Repair Worker, and Lucky Clover have been verified but have no DPS stat at all
(Farm/support types) — see [Loadout](/loadout/garden-tower-defense-best-loadout) for those.
Sunflower, Farmer, Watermelon, Peas In A Pod, Bamboo, Ghost Pepper, Drone, and Umbra have
lower verified DPS than the units above and are covered on the
[Early Game](/early-game/garden-tower-defense-best-early-game-units) and
[Plants](/plants/garden-tower-defense-best-plants) pages instead, to keep this table focused
on top performers.

## 待确认

- Whether any unverified unit outperforms Venus Flytrap is unknown — treat this list as a
  floor, not a ceiling, until more units are checked.
