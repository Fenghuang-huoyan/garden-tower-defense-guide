## Sort order: verified max DPS only, nothing else

This page ranks units strictly by their stated max DPS number from the wiki — no cost, no
obtainability weighting, just raw output. For a ranking that balances DPS against what it costs
to field, see [Best Units](/best-units/garden-tower-defense-best-units) instead; that page uses
a different sort and will show a different order.

Garden Tower Defense has 353 named units, and only a handful have been individually verified
against the game's wiki so far. This page ranks exactly those verified units — it will grow as
more get checked. We'd rather show a short, accurate list than a long one borrowed from
unsourced tier rankings.

## Ranked by verified max DPS (currently obtainable units only)

| Rank | Unit | Max DPS | How to get it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Venus Flytrap | 3750 | Enchanted Summon crate, 0.01% chance |
| 2 | Bouncing Shroom | 3200 (AoE/splash) | Greenhouse Summon, 0.005% chance |
| 3 | Rafflesia | 1500 (trap-type, attaches to mobs) | Mystery Trader shop, 20% stock chance |
| 4 | Beehive | ~900 (computed: 2250 damage ÷ 2.5s interval; wiki doesn't state DPS directly) | Bee Summon, 0.01% chance |

**Venus Flytrap has the highest verified DPS among units you can actually still get (3750).**
No longer-obtainable unit is included in this ranking — see below for why that matters.

## Units checked but excluded from this ranking

Money Tree, Sunflower, and Farmer have been verified (see [Best Units](/best-units/garden-tower-defense-best-units)
and [Early Game](/early-game/garden-tower-defense-best-early-game-units)) but Money Tree is a
Farm-type unit with no DPS stat, and Sunflower/Farmer's low-tier DPS numbers (397 and 102) don't
change the ranking above — they're left off this table to keep it focused on top performers.
