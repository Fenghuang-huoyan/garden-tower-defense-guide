## How to redeem

1. Launch Garden Tower Defense on Roblox.
2. Click the Shop button on the left (or press `F`).
3. Open the Codes section inside the Shop menu.
4. Enter the code exactly as shown (codes are case-sensitive).
5. Press Claim/Redeem to collect the reward.
