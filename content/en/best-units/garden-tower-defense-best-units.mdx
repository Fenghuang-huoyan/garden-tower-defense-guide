## Sort order: DPS balanced against cost, not raw DPS alone

This page ranks units by weighing max DPS against Cost Efficiency (DPS per 1000 spent) together
— a unit with lower raw DPS but much better value per point spent can rank above a higher-DPS
unit here. For a pure DPS-only ranking, see [Best DPS](/best-dps/garden-tower-defense-best-dps-units)
instead — the order there is different on purpose.

## Read this before chasing a "best unit" recommendation

Two of the units most commonly recommended as "the best" by third-party content are actually
**no longer obtainable**: Golem (limited exclusive, removed after its June 2025 event) and
Astraflora (limited stock of 10,101, sold out after its February 2026 event). If a guide tells
you to farm for one of these, you can't — they're gone. This page separates units you can
actually still get from ones that are historical strength references only.

## Currently obtainable, ranked by DPS + cost efficiency combined

| Rank | Unit | Rarity | Max DPS | Cost Efficiency (DPS per 1000 spent) | How to get it |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rafflesia | Godly | 1500 | **162.2** — highest verified so far | Mystery Trader shop, 20% stock chance |
| 2 | Venus Flytrap | Godly | 3750 | 71.9 | Enchanted Summon crate, 0.01% chance |
| 3 | Beehive | Godly | ~900 (computed) | 32.1 | Bee Summon, 0.01% chance |
| 4 | Bouncing Shroom | Godly | 3200 (splash/AoE) | 22.1 | Greenhouse Summon, 0.005% chance |

These aren't strictly "better/worse" in a single dimension — they fill different roles:

- **Rafflesia** is a trap unit (attaches to a mob as it passes, drops off on death, reattaches)
  with by far the best value per point spent of anything verified so far.
- **Venus Flytrap** has the best single-target cost efficiency among the pure-attacker units.
- **Beehive** is a spawner with infinite range — it summons bees rather than attacking directly,
  and the wiki itself flags its stat table as possibly out of date.
- **Bouncing Shroom** trades cost efficiency for area damage (bounces down the track, radius 9),
  making it better against grouped enemies rather than a single high-value target.

|---|---|---|---|---|

not a support/buff unit, despite some secondhand summaries describing it that way.
