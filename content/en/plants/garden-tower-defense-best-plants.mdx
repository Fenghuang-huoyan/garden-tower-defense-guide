## Sort order: none — this is a method page, not a ranking

Every unit in Garden Tower Defense is a plant, so "best plants" and "best units" ask almost the
same question. Rather than repeat a ranked table here (see [Best Units](/best-units/garden-tower-defense-best-units)
for that, sorted by DPS + cost efficiency, or [Best DPS](/best-dps/garden-tower-defense-best-dps-units)
for a pure-DPS sort), this page explains the method: what stats actually matter and why, using
verified examples that aren't presented in ranked order.

## The stat block every plant shares

Every unit page on the game's Fandom wiki reports the same fields, which means you can compare
any two plants on equal footing:

- **Rarity** — Common through Exclusive (7 tiers)
- **Currently Obtainable?** — a lot of frequently-recommended plants are actually discontinued
- **Placement Cost / Placement Limit** — how much it costs to place, and how many copies you
  can field at once
- **DPS, Damage, Range, Attack Speed** — given as a level-1 → max-level range
- **Cost Efficiency (DPS per 1000 spent)** — the wiki's own built-in value-for-money metric

## Reading cost efficiency with real examples

| Plant | Cost Efficiency (DPS/1000) | Obtainable? | Takeaway |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rafflesia | 162.2 | Yes | Highest verified efficiency, but it's a trap (attaches to mobs), not a straightforward attacker |
| Venus Flytrap | 71.9 | Yes | High single-target value per point spent |
| Golem | 52 | No | Decent efficiency, but you literally cannot get it anymore |
| Bouncing Shroom | 22.1 | Yes | Low raw efficiency, but adds splash/AoE damage a single-target number doesn't capture |

The lesson: a high Cost Efficiency number only matters if the plant is actually obtainable, and
a lower number isn't automatically worse if the plant covers a role (like AoE clear, or a trap
mechanic like Rafflesia's) that a pure single-target attacker can't.
