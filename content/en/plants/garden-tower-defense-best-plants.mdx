## Sort order: none — this is a method page, not a ranking

Every unit in Garden Tower Defense is a plant, so "best plants" and "best units" ask almost the
same question. Rather than repeat a ranked table here (see [Best Units](/best-units/garden-tower-defense-best-units)
for one sorted by DPS + cost efficiency, or [Best DPS](/best-dps/garden-tower-defense-best-dps-units)
for a pure-DPS sort), this page explains the method: what stats actually matter and why a raw
DPS number can be misleading, using examples not featured on those other pages.

## The stat block every plant shares

Every unit page on the game's Fandom wiki reports the same fields, which means you can compare
any two plants on equal footing:

- **Rarity** — Common through Exclusive (7 tiers)
- **Currently Obtainable?** — a lot of frequently-recommended plants are actually discontinued
- **Placement Cost / Placement Limit** — how much it costs to place, and how many copies you
  can field at once
- **DPS, Damage, Range, Attack Speed** — given as a level-1 → max-level range
- **Cost Efficiency (DPS per 1000 spent)** — a value-for-money metric the wiki states for some
  (not all) units

## Why a raw DPS number doesn't tell the whole story

| Plant | DPS-only picture | What the DPS number misses |
|---|---|---|
| Ghost Pepper | 6 → 177 (looks weak) | Also applies fire damage-over-time separately from its listed Damage stat — the DPS figure undersells it |
| Umbra | 30 → 300 (mid-tier at best) | Can attack hidden/stealthed enemies that other units can't target at all — a utility role no DPS number captures |
| Watermelon | 44 → 480 | Deals damage in bursts (1x up to 10x multiplier) rather than a steady rate, so its effective output spikes unevenly |
| Money Tree | No DPS stat at all | It's not supposed to fight — it generates +20 to +65 Seeds income per cycle instead |

The lesson: before dismissing a plant for a low DPS number, check whether it has a
damage-over-time effect, a detection ability, burst mechanics, or an economy role that a single
number doesn't represent.
