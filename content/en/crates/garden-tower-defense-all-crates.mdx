## Classic Summon, in detail

The starter crate, in the game since its original June 2025 launch. Bought from Gerald Gnome
in the Summon Shop for 100 (single pull) or 900 (ten-pull).

| Rarity | Chance |
|---|---|
| Common | 20% |
| Uncommon | 15–17% |
| Rare | 10–11% |
| Legendary | 4–5% |
| Mythical | 0.99–1% |
| Godly | 0.01% |

18 units are currently obtainable from it: Cactus, Tomato (Common); Farmer, Gnome, Potato,
Pineapple Cannon (Uncommon); Mushroom, Chili Pepper, Money Tree, Bamboo, Rose Trap, Baby
Carrots (Rare); Palm Tree, Broccoli (Epic); Peas In A Pod, Watermelon, Sunflower (Legendary);
Bloodvine (Godly).

Unconfirmed — the wiki's own rarity chance table labels one tier "Mythical" while the individual
unit pages (e.g. Sunflower) label the same rarity "Legendary" at a matching 0.99% rate. This
looks like a naming change the wiki hasn't fully reconciled; we're reporting it as-is rather
than guessing which label is current.

## Unconfirmed
