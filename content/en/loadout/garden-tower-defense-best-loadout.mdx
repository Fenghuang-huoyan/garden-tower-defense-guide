## Sort order: role coverage, not a numeric ranking

Unlike the [Best DPS](/best-dps/garden-tower-defense-best-dps-units) and
[Best Units](/best-units/garden-tower-defense-best-units) pages, this page doesn't rank units
by a number — a loadout is about covering different jobs at once. Below are the five verified
roles that currently exist among checked units, so you can see what a balanced pick looks like.

## We couldn't verify a specific "best loadout" — here's why

Most results for this search are short-form videos (TikTok clips titled things like "Best PvP
Loadout") with no way to independently confirm a title, publish date, or the actual in-game
result shown. Without that, we can't cite them as evidence for a specific team combo, and we
found no official Discord or developer loadout guide either. Rather than repeat an unverified
list of unit names pulled from a search summary, this page sticks to what's actually confirmed.

## What we do know: loadouts are constrained by Placement Limit

Every unit has a maximum number you can field at once — you can't just pick your three highest-DPS
units and stack them freely.

| Unit | Placement Limit |
|---|---|
| Golem | 5 |
| Venus Flytrap | 4 |
| Bouncing Shroom | 4 |
| Astraflora | 2 |

## Verified roles among currently-obtainable units

| Role | Unit | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Single-target DPS | Venus Flytrap | High cost-efficiency single-target damage |
| AoE / crowd clear | Bouncing Shroom | Bounces down the track, splash damage to groups |
| Trap / lockdown | Rafflesia | Attaches to a mob as it passes, drops and reattaches on death |
| Spawner / passive | Beehive | Infinite range, summons bees rather than attacking directly |

## Roles that currently have no obtainable unit filling them

- **Crowd control (stun)** — Golem stunned enemies on hit, but it's no longer obtainable. No
  currently-obtainable unit with a documented stun ability has been verified yet.
- **Multi-target piercing** — Astraflora's beam hit multiple enemies in a line, but it's also
  no longer obtainable.

A sound loadout mixes roles (single-target, AoE, trap, spawner) rather than stacking copies of
one unit — but we don't yet have verified evidence for which specific 3-to-5-unit combination
performs best in practice.
