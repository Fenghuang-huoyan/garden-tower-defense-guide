## Sort order: role coverage, not a numeric ranking

Unlike the [Best DPS](/best-dps/garden-tower-defense-best-dps-units) and
[Best Units](/best-units/garden-tower-defense-best-units) pages, this page doesn't rank units
by a number — a loadout is about covering different jobs at once. This is the only page on this
site that includes units with no DPS stat at all, since a support unit can be worth a loadout
slot without ever dealing damage.

## Loadouts are constrained by Placement Limit

Every unit has a maximum number you can field at once — you can't just pick your highest-value
units and stack them freely.

## Seven verified roles among currently-obtainable units

| Role | Unit | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Single-target DPS | Venus Flytrap | High cost-efficiency single-target damage |
| AoE / crowd clear | Bouncing Shroom | Bounces down the track, splash damage to groups |
| Trap / lockdown | Rafflesia | Attaches to a mob as it passes, drops and reattaches on death |
| Spawner / passive | Beehive | Infinite range, summons bees rather than attacking directly |
| Crowd control (stun) | Stun Flower | Stuns enemies on hit, stopping them in place |
| Support (damage buff) | Lucky Clover | +10% damage and bonus crit chance for all units in range — no direct DPS itself |
| Base sustain (healing) | Repair Worker | Regenerates base health over time, up to a 1,000,000 cap — also no DPS stat |
| Detection / anti-stealth | Umbra | Can attack enemies that are hidden from other units |

That's a genuine spread across offense, control, support, and utility — enough to build a
loadout that covers more than one job, using only currently-obtainable units.

## A role Golem used to fill, and still doesn't have a great replacement

Golem's whole kit was a stun ability, but it's no longer obtainable. Stun Flower now fills the
crowd-control role instead — worth noting its stun duration (1.197s) and cooldown (3s) are
different numbers from Golem's old 0.5s stun, so it's not a like-for-like swap, just the closest
verified currently-obtainable option.

## Second source: a tested starter pick, cross-checked against a real video

Per our sourcing standard, a ranking-adjacent claim needs either objective in-game numbers
(above) or a named, dated creator video with a stated result — not a vague "many players say."
We checked one: **"Top 3 Best Starter Units for All Gamemodes in Garden Tower Defense"** by
RDO (30,470 views, published March 27, 2026, timestamped chapters for PvP/Survival/Classic/Endless).

In the PvP chapter, the creator ranks Glowthorn as his third-best PvP starter pick, describing
it as a **"full electric"** unit doing roughly 3,500 DPS, and explains it isn't ranked higher
only because a starter slot is better spent on a cheaper early pick.

This supports one concrete, checkable claim: **Glowthorn is a real, named starter pick a tested
creator ranks highly for PvP specifically**, with a stated DPS figure (3,500) in the video. We
have not independently verified that 3,500 DPS number against the Fandom wiki (Glowthorn is not
yet in our checked-unit pool), so treat it as creator-reported, not wiki-confirmed — but it's a
genuine, dated, named source, not an anonymous "best loadout" claim.
