## How many units are there?

Counting directly from the game's Fandom wiki roster (not an estimate), Garden Tower Defense
currently lists **353 units across 7 rarity tiers**:

| Rarity | Unit count |
|---|---|
| Common | 17 |
| Uncommon | 26 |
| Rare | 35 |
| Epic | 34 |
| Legendary | 31 |
| Godly | 59 |
| Exclusive | 151 |
| **Total** | **353** |

Exclusive is by far the largest tier, made up mostly of limited-time and seasonal units
(including a large "Rainbow" recolor series and holiday-themed variants).

## Exclusive (151)

The largest and most volatile tier — mostly limited-time, seasonal, and event-exclusive units.
A representative sample, cross-checked against real trade listings on Traderie to confirm
these are genuine in-game items: Golem, Blossom Barrage, Hellroot, Doompetal, Carrotastrophe,
Timekeeper, Rosebeam, Auranji, Fisherman, Nightshade, Electric Beetroot, Grapes, Big Mushroom,
Corn, Juggercorn, Earth Dragon, Astral Blossom, Voltshade, Elemental Bloom, plus a large
"Rainbow" recolor series (Rainbow Tomato, Rainbow Bamboo, Rainbow Pumpkin, and more).

Unconfirmed — the full Exclusive-tier list runs well past 100 more named units beyond this sample;
see the wiki's Towers page directly for the complete roster.

## What's still unconfirmed

- Per-unit DPS/cost/range stats are only confirmed for two units so far (Golem, Venus Flytrap —
  see the [Tier List](/tier-list/garden-tower-defense-tier-list) page). Verifying all 353
  individually was not attempted this pass.
- The wiki itself flags that some unit images are incomplete, meaning it's still being actively
  edited by the community — newer units may be missing or miscategorized.
