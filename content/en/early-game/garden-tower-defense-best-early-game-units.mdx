## Start with the Classic Summon, not the rare crates

New players can only realistically pull from the **Classic Summon** (100 currency for one pull,
900 for ten) — the starter crate that's been in the game since launch. Godly and Exclusive
crates aren't a realistic early-game source. Here's what's actually confirmed obtainable from it.

## Farmer (Uncommon) — common early pull

- Drop rate from Classic Summon: **15–17%** — you'll likely get one in your first few pulls.
- DPS: 8 → 102, Damage: 7 → 49, Range: 6 → 10, Attack Speed: 0.90 → 0.49
- Placement Cost: 200 — cheap enough to field several early on.

## Sunflower (Legendary) — rare but still reachable from the starter crate

- Drop rate from Classic Summon: **0.99%** — much rarer than Farmer, but notable because it
  doesn't require a premium crate, just luck on the cheapest one.
- DPS: 32 → 397, Placement Cost: 1200, Placement Limit: 8
- In the game since its original April 2025 launch version.
