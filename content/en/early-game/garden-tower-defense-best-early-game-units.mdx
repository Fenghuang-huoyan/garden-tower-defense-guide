## Sort order: Classic Summon drop rate, not power

New players can only realistically pull from the **Classic Summon** (100 currency for one pull,
900 for ten) — the starter crate that's been in the game since launch. Godly and Exclusive
crates aren't a realistic early-game source. This page is sorted by how likely you are to pull
each unit, not by how strong it is — see [Best DPS](/best-dps/garden-tower-defense-best-dps-units)
or [Best Units](/best-units/garden-tower-defense-best-units) for strength-based rankings.

## Six units confirmed obtainable from the Classic Summon, by drop rate

| Unit | Rarity | Drop Rate | Max DPS | Placement Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Farmer | Uncommon | 15–17% | 102 | 200 |
| Bamboo | Rare | 10% | 150 | 500 |
| Broccoli | Epic | 1% | 667 | 800 |
| Watermelon | Legendary | 1% | 480 | 1000 |
| Peas In A Pod | Legendary | 1% | 375 | 1000 |
| Sunflower | Legendary | 0.99% | 397 | 1200 |

You'll likely land Farmer within your first few pulls — it's cheap enough to field several
copies right away. Everything else on this list has roughly a 1-in-100 (or worse) chance per
pull, but none of them require a premium crate, just luck on the cheapest one.

Also worth calling out: **Palm Tree** (Epic, 4–5% drop rate, max DPS 454, Placement Cost 500)
sits between Bamboo and the 1%-tier units — a solid mid-rarity pull with a better-than-1% shot.

## Unconfirmed

- The wiki's own Sunflower page has a minor inconsistency between its overview damage figures
  and its upgrade table's level-1 value — not resolved yet.
- Many base Common-rarity units (e.g. Tomato) don't have a filled-in wiki page yet, so their
  exact stats can't be confirmed — the wiki is still being actively written by the community.
- No official beginner guide or verified creator video was found for a specific early-game
  strategy beyond "what you can pull."
