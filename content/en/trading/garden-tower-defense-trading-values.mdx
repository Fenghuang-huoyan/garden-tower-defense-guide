## Looking for specific numbers?

Check the [Value List](/values/garden-tower-defense-value-list) page for a sourced snapshot of
item values. This page is about something different: how trading actually happens and how to
judge whether a deal is fair, since repeating the same value table here wouldn't add anything.

## There's no official in-game trading documentation yet

We checked the game's Fandom wiki for a "Trading," "Trade System," or "How to Trade" page —
all three exist as titles but have no written content yet. That means the exact in-game steps
for completing a trade haven't been documented by an official or community source we could
verify, and we're not going to invent them.

## What is confirmed: trading happens on third-party platforms

Actual trade activity is visible on **Traderie** (traderie.com/gardentowerdefense), a
third-party marketplace where real players post barter-style listings ("1x Item A for 1x Item
B"), each with a username and date. This confirms which items are genuinely being traded, but
Traderie doesn't display a standardized price, so it can't tell you an exact numeric value on
its own — pair it with the [Value List](/values/garden-tower-defense-value-list) page's tracker
data for that.

## How to judge a fair trade

- Cross-check an item's value across more than one tracker site rather than trusting a single
  source — different community trackers can disagree, sometimes significantly.
- Confirm the item actually appears in real trade listings (not just a value-list entry) before
  trusting a quoted price.
- Be skeptical of a deal that only one side's source supports.
