## What the result means

- **Value score** is the source tracker's scale. It is not a verified conversion to Seeds,
  Robux or cash. The shorthand `k` is expanded to 1,000 score points for arithmetic only.
- **Their score minus yours** compares the displayed baselines. A positive number means
  their displayed score is higher, not that a trade is guaranteed to be profitable.
- Entries ending in **+** are approximate. The tool preserves that warning rather than
  treating the difference as an exact price or a win/loss verdict.
- Unlisted items cannot be priced by this dataset. Do not replace a missing value with zero.
  For a missing item, check the source and compare actual recent offers separately.

## Before confirming a trade

1. Check the [dated Value List](/values/garden-tower-defense-value-list) and its source.
2. Compare recent offers for the same item and variant. A listing is not a completed sale.
3. Consider demand and availability as well as the displayed score. The source can stay
   unchanged while actual trading conditions change.
4. Recheck scarce or expensive items using more than one dated source. The tracker is
   community-run and is not an official developer price list.

The tool has no direct market feed and does not claim daily price verification. The exact
currency denomination and the tracker's methodology remain unconfirmed.
